# IsoRim Waylith - VGE Pack

[Vanilla Gravship Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3609835606) in the 3D view of [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) and on the floors of [IsoRimZ](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-ProjectZ) (RimWorld 1.7).

**3D models for VGE (Chapter 1 and 2)**
- About 390 models: gravship furniture, consoles, thrusters, shields, oxygen and fuel parts, crates and small props
- Ship guns on raised frames that rise through the roof, with shots and lasers leaving the barrel
- Enemy gravships and their parts, mech turrets and structures, destroyed variants
- VGE mechs, insects and the wormspitter; vacsuits and helmets, dyeable like vanilla gear
- VGE armour hull corners stand in 3D and fly with the ship
- Enemy gravships fly in as 3D ships, with thruster flames, engine glow and dust

**Floors with IsoRimZ (optional)**
- VGE subarmor and subscaffold can be built on upper decks; VGE artillery damages decks, and they can be repaired
- Enemy gravships and "The Gravship" scenario land on the ground; artillery, salvagers and turrets work across floors
- Vac barrier roof colours and fog stay right on upper decks

Requires IsoRim Waylith (latest version), IsoRim Waylith - VE Framework Pack and Vanilla Gravship Expanded - Chapter 1. Chapter 2 and IsoRimZ are optional. Load after all of them. With an older IsoRim Waylith the pack still loads, with fewer models.

让 Vanilla Gravship Expanded 在 IsoRim Waylith 的 3D 视角和 IsoRimZ 的楼层上正常工作。

**VGE 的 3D 模型（第一章和第二章）**
- 约 390 个模型：逆重飞船家具、控制台、推进器、护盾、氧气和燃料设施、箱子和小摆件
- 舰炮装在高架上，从屋顶伸出，炮弹和激光从炮口射出
- 敌方逆重飞船和它们的部件、机械族炮塔和建筑、毁坏版本
- VGE 的机械族、虫族和外星蠕虫；真空服和头盔，可以像原版装备一样染色
- VGE 装甲船体的拐角显示为 3D，并随飞船一起起飞
- 敌方飞船以 3D 形式飞来降落，带推进器火焰、引擎光晕和扬尘

**IsoRimZ 楼层支持（可选）**
- 可以在上层甲板上建造 VGE 下层装甲和下层脚手架；VGE 火炮会打坏甲板，甲板可以修复
- 敌方飞船和"飞船"剧本降落在地面上；火炮、拾荒者和炮塔可以跨楼层正常工作
- 上层甲板的真空屏障屋顶颜色和战争迷雾保持正确

需要最新版 IsoRim Waylith、IsoRim Waylith - VE Framework Pack 和 Vanilla Gravship Expanded - Chapter 1。Chapter 2 和 IsoRimZ 可选。请放在它们之后加载。IsoRim Waylith 版本较旧时也能加载，只是模型会少一些。

Vanilla Gravship Expanded is made by the Vanilla Expanded team (Oskar Potocki and others). The models are new, made after VGE's art; this pack contains no files of the original mod (the damaged deck uses VGE's own texture by path).
