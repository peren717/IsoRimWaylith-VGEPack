# IsoRim Waylith - VGE Pack

[Vanilla Gravship Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3609835606) in the 3D view of [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) and on the floors of [IsoRimZ](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-ProjectZ) (RimWorld 1.7).

- VGE armour hull corners stand in 3D and fly with the ship.
- Enemy gravships and "The Gravship" scenario land on the ground; VGE artillery and salvagers stay on the right floor. VGE turrets aim across floors.
- Vac barrier roof colours and fog stay right on upper decks.
- VGE subarmor and subscaffold can be built on upper decks; VGE artillery damages decks, and they can be repaired.

Requires IsoRim Waylith (Api 26 or newer) and Vanilla Gravship Expanded - Chapter 1. Chapter 2 and IsoRimZ are optional: the floor fixes load only with IsoRimZ (Api 4 or newer). Load after all of them.

让 Vanilla Gravship Expanded 在 IsoRim Waylith 的 3D 视角和 IsoRimZ 的楼层上正常工作。

- VGE 装甲船体的拐角显示为 3D，并随飞船一起起飞。
- 敌方飞船和"飞船"剧本降落在地面上；VGE 火炮和拾荒者都在正确的楼层。VGE 炮塔可以跨楼层瞄准。
- 上层甲板的真空屏障屋顶颜色和战争迷雾保持正确。
- 可以在上层甲板上建造 VGE 下层装甲和下层脚手架；VGE 火炮会打坏甲板，甲板可以修复。

需要 IsoRim Waylith（Api 26 或更新）和 Vanilla Gravship Expanded - Chapter 1。Chapter 2 和 IsoRimZ 可选：楼层修复只在有 IsoRimZ（Api 4 或更新）时加载。请放在它们之后加载。
